Lyrics → Pro7

Paste your lyrics and get a ProPresenter 7 song in the same style as your template. Tamil text is converted to the Alankaram Plain (Bamini) font encoding for you.

Leave a blank line between sections. Put a label such as Pallavi or Verse 2 on the first line of a section to name its group. Unlabelled sections are called Verse 1, Verse 2 and so on. The song name is filled in automatically from the first line, written in English letters. You can change it. Lyrics that are already in Bamini text are used as they are.

Before you import. Install the Alankaram Plain font on the computer running ProPresenter 7. In ProPresenter 7 choose File, then Import File, and select the .pro file.

Check the first song. Letters with the long “ூ” sign, and a few rare ones, are not covered yet. The page lists any it could not convert. Everything runs in your browser; your lyrics are not uploaded anywhere.
